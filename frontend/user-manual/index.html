Meet the Demo Project

In Arco's Project Explorer, expand Test/Demos and double-click ABC_Group_Quarterly_Review_202608. On the Project Page, select ABC Auto → PPA → NJ → Exclusive → BI Full Tort.

  • Input: Gross Loss--Paid, displayed cumulatively.
  • DFM examples: F 13 - Paid DFM w/ Selected LDFs and F 23 - Incurred DFM w/ Selected LDFs, using the corresponding paid and reported loss triangles.
  • Result Selection example: F 91 - Current Qtr Indicated, combining the two DFM outputs with equal weights.
  • Ratio basis: Earned Premium.
  • Periods: 12-month origins and development intervals, with origin years 2017–2026.
  • Valuation: August 2026. The development ages run from 8 to 116 months in 12-month steps.
Practice with illustrative data

All amounts are illustrative training values, and Jordan Lee is a fictional reviewer. Use a distinct method Name, such as My Paid DFM, to compare your own selections with the finished examples.

Use This Guide Beside Arco

  1. In Arco, choose Help → User Guide. The guide opens in your browser from the server connected to the app.
  2. Keep the guide beside Arco and follow one task at a time. Use the chapter navigation and On This Page links to return to a step.
  3. Click a screenshot to enlarge it. Press Esc or choose Close image to return.
  4. Use Next step and Previous step to move through a screenshot sequence. Printing includes every step.

For a first review, follow Project Page → Development Factor Method → Result Selection.
